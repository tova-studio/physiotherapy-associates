What we treat

Our Services

Care for every stage of recovery, from first injury to getting back to work and sport.

  • I.

    Musculoskeletal Injury Rehabilitation

    Comprehensive assessment and evidence-based rehabilitation to reduce pain, restore movement, and improve function.

  • II.

    Post Surgical Rehabilitation

    Individualised rehabilitation programmes designed to restore strength, mobility, and confidence following surgery.

  • III.

    Sports Injuries

    Expert assessment, treatment, and rehabilitation to help you recover safely and return to your sport.

  • IV.

    Manipulative Therapy

    Hands-on treatment techniques to improve joint mobility, reduce pain, and restore normal movement.

  • V.

    Pain Management

    Personalised treatment strategies to help manage pain and improve your quality of life.

  • VI.

    Return to Work Programmes

    Graduated rehabilitation programmes to help you return to work safely and confidently.

About the clinic

Physiotherapy Care You Can Trust

Physiotherapy Associates is committed to providing high-quality, patient-focused physiotherapy care to the Christchurch community.

From sports injuries and post-surgical recovery to workplace injuries and long-term pain, we tailor every treatment to your recovery. We're an ACC registered provider, so ACC claims can be lodged directly at the clinic.

Our physiotherapists

Meet Our Team

Keroles Abousif

Physiotherapist & Director

Keroles moved to New Zealand in 2007 after growing up near Cairo, Egypt.

He graduated from Otago Physiotherapy in 2022 after completing his final placement in Blenheim.

Keroles has worked with club rugby union teams, the Timaru Boys’ High School First XV, representative South Canterbury rugby teams, and the ITF tennis tournaments held in Timaru, providing physiotherapy support to players.

His interests include sports injuries, acute injuries, and post-surgical rehabilitation.

Wendy Cooper

Physiotherapist

Dip. Phys. (Otago) Ad. Dip. Phys. (Orthopaedic Manual Therapy) (Auckland)

Wendy has extensive experience working in New Zealand, the UK, and Saudi Arabia.

Her interests include manual therapy and long-term fitness and health.
